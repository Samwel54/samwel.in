About Me

I am an MTech scholar in Biotechnology at IIT Guwahati with a focus on bio-formulations, dryland afforestation, and applied computational biology. My research centers on sustainable biotechnological solutions to ecological and environmental challenges.

Research Focus

  • Plant Growth-Promoting Rhizobacteria (PGPR) & Biostimulants: Formulation and bio-evaluation for enhanced soil and crop health.
  • Dryland Afforestation: Application of hydrogel matrices and biostimulants for moisture retention and eco-restoration.
  • Computational Biology & Applied Biotechnology: Protein structure modeling, bioinformatics, and biological data analysis.
