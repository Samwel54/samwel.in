Laboratory & Fieldwork Initiatives

Ongoing Research

Project 01 • Dryland Afforestation

1. Hydrogel Matrix Formulations & PGPR Biostimulants for Dryland Survival

Hydrogel-assisted dryland afforestation is a crucial biotechnological intervention for restoring degraded soils in semi-arid environments. My research focuses on synthesizing swellable hydrogel matrices embedded with Plant Growth-Promoting Rhizobacteria (PGPR) and biostimulants to improve soil moisture retention and seed germination under water-deficit stress.

These bio-formulations provide a sustained-release micro-environment for microbial survival, assisting root colonization and nutrient availability during critical early establishment phases.

Hydrogel Synthesis PGPR Inoculation Soil Moisture Retention
Soil matrix evaluation & plant growth assay testing
Bio-fertilizer isolation & rhizobacteria screening
Project 02 • Applied Microbiology

2. Plant-Microbe Interaction Assays & Bio-Evaluation

Assessing beneficial bacterial isolates for nitrogen fixation, phosphate solubilization, and siderophore production. By combining microbiological screening with greenhouse bio-evaluations, we quantify plant biomass accumulation, root architecture modulation, and abiotic stress mitigation.

These evaluations help develop robust, eco-friendly bio-fertilizers tailored for degraded soil restoration across Northeast India.

Phosphate Solubilization Bio-Assays Ecology
Project 03 • Computational Biology

3. Computational Protein Homology Modeling & Structural Analyses

Utilizing bioinformatics software pipelines including MODELLER and structural analysis tools to perform protein homology modeling and molecular docking. This computational arm complements our experimental wet-lab assays by predicting key enzyme interactions involved in stress response pathways.

Protein structure prediction & docking visualization
